<h2>Overview</h2>
To implement and test high-performance digital logic, I executed the complete FPGA design flow using Xilinx Vivado. This project involved developing robust VHDL modules from the ground up and managing the entire hardware pipeline—from initial RTL synthesis through to final bitstream generation for physical deployment.

<h2>Functional Verification</h2>
To guarantee the reliability of the hardware logic prior to physical implementation, I authored comprehensive verification testbenches. This rigorous testing phase was critical for validating the designs, ensuring cycle-accurate behavior, and achieving complete logic coverage before moving to the synthesis stage.

<h2>Synthesis & Performance Optimization</h2>
Following successful verification, I conducted deep-dive timing analysis and resource utilization checks on the synthesized designs. By carefully evaluating and tuning the logic, I optimized the physical footprint of the modules on the FPGA fabric and ensured the system successfully met all strict clock frequency constraints required for deployment.
